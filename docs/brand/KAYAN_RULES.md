# Kayan Maker Lab — agreed brand & content rules

Source: the owner's file «Kayan Maker Lab: المهارات والإضافات» (2026-11-15). These rules apply
to every post, story, Reel, video and caption made for Kayan. They extend the account skills
`instagram-3d-printing-publisher` and `kayan-brand-posters`; when they disagree, these rules win.

**Owner instruction (2026-11-15): apply these rules to new work only. Do not go back and edit
existing deliverables (published/delivered reels, posts, the website) unless the owner asks for
that specific item.**

- **Dialect:** Lebanese colloquial (عامية لبنانية), not formal Arabic — in designs and captions.
- **Website:** `kayanmakerlab.com` (written without `https://` in designs).
  **WhatsApp:** `81424535` (link `wa.me/96181424535`).
- **Services to mention:** the shop (ready-made parts); send us a photo; we model your idea;
  upload an STL to the calculator and see the price instantly. Delivery $5 anywhere in Lebanon,
  payment on delivery, Whish deposit for orders over $10. **Confirmed by the owner
  2026-11-15 for all new content** — do not write «الدفع عبر Whish Money» as the payment method
  (older deliverables that say so stay as they are).
- **Icons:** line icons, one colour only — cyan `#1DE5F5`. No coloured emoji in designs.
- **Logo:** the real Kayan logo (from the PDF / the website's `logo-mark` + `logo-word`), medium
  size (68 px tall on posts), no effects on the logo itself.
- **Carousel & Reel covers:** the full logo centred inside a cyan ring with 4 dots.
- **Profile picture:** the full logo on `#0F1A1E` with a light grid, no ring.
- **Every product gets 3 versions:** Instagram 1080×1350 (2 slides); Story 1080×1920;
  TikTok 1080×1920 (170 px clear on the right, 440 px clear at the bottom).
- **Hashtags:** 5 only, always including `#KayanMakerLab`.
- **Files (owner's PC):** `Desktop\kayan\kayan maker lab\designs` (posts, highlights, profile,
  `_template`); scripts and covers in `videos\scripts` and `videos\covers`.
- **Publishing:** never without explicit approval. The built-in browser can't upload files, so
  publishing is from the phone, with Claude in Chrome (can upload), or Meta Business Suite for
  Stories.
- **Brand colours:** cyan `#1DE5F5`, background `#0F1A1E` (charcoal teal-black) with a thin grid.
  Tag: «3D Printing» only (no CNC, no Laser).
- **Images for videos and designs (owner, 2026-11-16):** don't fall back on the plain shop
  photos or screenshots as the main visuals. When a scene needs a picture, write Nano Banana /
  Gemini prompts (Kayan style: dark `#0F1A1E` scene, cyan `#1DE5F5` neon/rim light, realistic
  3D-printed objects with visible layer lines, no text/logos) and **ask the owner to generate
  them and send them back**; then build with those images. Real website footage is still fine
  where the website itself is the subject.
- **How pictures appear in videos (owner, agreed 2026-11-18 — apply every time, don't ask again):**
  pictures go in the **poster-style frame** (`"frame"`: logo-shaped cut corners, offset outline,
  + marks, ~1000 px wide, `top` 590), **never full-bleed with text or bands over them**. All text
  (kicker, headline, subtitle pill) sits **above** the frame at a fixed position, with the accent
  bars between text and picture. Nothing (particles, sweep, pills, labels) is drawn over the
  product; use `pos` to keep the product centred in the frame.
- **Every new video gets new pictures (owner, 2026-11-20):** don't reuse pictures from earlier
  reels and don't start building with old ones. First send the owner Google Flow / Nano Banana
  prompts for every picture the video needs (vertical 9:16, Kayan style, subject centred for the
  poster frame), wait for the generated pictures, then build.
