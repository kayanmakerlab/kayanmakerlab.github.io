# Reel: «خمّن شو هيدي؟» (30 s) — no voice, made for music

Guess-the-piece game: each round starts zoomed 4x on a detail (croissant charm, pixel heart, QR stand card holder, dallah), headline «شو هيدي؟», then pulls back at 2.6 s into the round to reveal the whole piece in the poster frame with its name. Outro asks «كم عرفت؟ اكتبلنا بالتعليقات» for engagement. Pictures: the owner's generated keychain and QR-stand pictures. Rounds start at 2.5, 7.5, 12.5, 17.5 s; reveals at 5.1, 10.1, 15.1, 20.1 s; outro at 22.5 s.
